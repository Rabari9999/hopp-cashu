<p>Hopp is a podcast player for every podcast, built for Podcasting 2.0 and value for value. No account, no tracking, no server of its own: install it and listen.</p>

<p>Connect your own Lightning wallet and boost the shows you love, or stream sats while you listen – Hopp never holds your sats and takes no cut. With an optional Nostr identity you comment on episodes and keep your devices in sync, encrypted.</p>

<p><b>Listening</b></p>
<ul>
<li>✅ Home with what is new and your playlist, library as cards or a list, sorted with tags of your own</li>
<li>✅ A playlist – what plays on top, what comes next below, reorderable – downloads on Wi-Fi, sleep timer, skip silence, your own skip intervals</li>
<li>✅ Speed, intro and outro skipping for each show; quiet shows made louder</li>
<li>✅ Chapters with pictures, transcripts that read along, people, show notes, clips</li>
<li>✅ Tap a person to see every episode with them</li>
<li>✅ Listening statistics: time, time saved, top shows and episodes, sats given</li>
<li>✅ Home screen widget, lock screen and Android Auto</li>
<li>✅ Share Hopp's link, the website, the audio link or the file itself</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Value for value</b></p>
<ul>
<li>✅ Your own Lightning wallet over Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC) – no built-in wallet</li>
<li>✅ Boost an episode or a whole show, quick boost from the lock screen, stream sats per minute</li>
<li>✅ A daily budget you set, payment history with who got what</li>
<li>✅ 100 % to the podcasters, routing fees shown apart; supporting Hopp is your choice</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Nostr</b></p>
<ul>
<li>✅ Optional and off until you turn it on: see what your Nostr contacts listen to, boost and comment on; share your subscriptions with mutual contacts, sealed so only they can read them</li>
<li>✅ Comments on every episode, Fountain's included: write, like and zap – publicly, privately or anonymously</li>
<li>✅ Identity made in Hopp, imported or kept in Amber or another signer; profile name and picture set in Hopp; your own relays</li>
<li>✅ Sync between your devices: subscriptions, progress, favourites, playlist, settings, statistics – encrypted, no Hopp server</li>
<li>✅ Restore your library on a new device</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Discover</b></p>
<ul>
<li>✅ Podcast Index: search, categories, your shows' recommendations, new shows that take sats, popular right now</li>
<li>✅ Optionally fyyd and Apple, a feed address, OPML import and export – moving over from another app is easy</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Sovereigncast shows</b></p>
<ul>
<li>✅ Release Boosting, Boost2Unlock, topic suggestions and votes, top boosts and top episodes</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy and more</b></p>
<ul>
<li>✅ A privacy page: who sees what when you listen, and what you can do about it</li>
<li>✅ Notifications for boosts and zaps, set for each on its own</li>
<li>✅ Phones and tablets, upright and sideways, light and dark, English and German; Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese as a preview</li>
</ul>

<p>Hopp is open source (MIT).</p>
