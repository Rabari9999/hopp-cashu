<p>Hopp is a podcast player for every podcast, built for Podcasting 2.0: chapters, transcripts, people, and value for value over Lightning.</p>

<p><b>No account, no tracking.</b> Install it and listen. Hopp collects nothing and runs no server of its own. A privacy page explains in plain words who sees what when you listen — and what you can do about it.</p>

<p><b>Only your wallet.</b> Hopp has no built-in wallet and never holds your sats. Connect your own Lightning wallet over Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC), boost an episode or a whole show, or stream sats while you listen — within a daily budget you set.</p>

<p><b>100 % to the podcasters.</b> Hopp takes no cut; routing fees are shown separately. Supporting Hopp is a visible option you turn on yourself.</p>

<p><b>Comments over Nostr.</b> Every episode's comments, Fountain's included. With a Nostr identity — made in Hopp, imported, or kept in Amber — write, like and zap them, publicly, privately or anonymously, post a boost as a comment, and use your own relays.</p>

<p><b>Discover</b> shows by category, from your shows' recommendations, new shows that take sats and what is popular right now — from the Podcast Index.</p>

<p><b>Sovereigncast shows</b> come alive in Hopp: Release Boosting, Boost2Unlock, topic votes and the community's top boosts.</p>

<ul>
<li>Library as cards or a list, Home with what is new and what you are listening to</li>
<li>Find shows in the Podcast Index (optionally fyyd and Apple), by feed address or OPML</li>
<li>Queue, downloads on Wi-Fi, sleep timer, speed per show, skip silence, your own skip intervals</li>
<li>Notifications for boosts and zaps, set for each on its own</li>
<li>Android Auto, phones and tablets, in English and German</li>
</ul>

<p>Hopp is open source (MIT).</p>
